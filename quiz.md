# Mini Quiz

1. An unexpected message urgently asks for your one-time code. What do you do?
   - A. Send it
   - B. Do not share it; verify independently and report it
   - **Answer: B**
2. Does HTTPS guarantee a site is legitimate?
   - A. Yes
   - B. No
   - **Answer: B**
3. Safest way to reach your bank?
   - A. Use an unexpected email link
   - B. Type the known official address or use a trusted bookmark
   - **Answer: B**
4. Does a “Lower signal” checker result guarantee safety?
   - A. Yes
   - B. No
   - **Answer: B**
