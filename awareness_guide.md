# Phishing Awareness Guide

Phishing is a social-engineering attempt to trick people into sharing credentials, sending money, opening harmful attachments, or visiting deceptive sites.

## Warning signs
- Unexpected urgency, threats, prizes, or payment requests.
- Sender address or domain subtly differs from the real organization.
- Link destination does not match the visible text.
- Requests for passwords, one-time codes, recovery codes, or payment details.
- Unexpected attachments or requests to enable macros.

## Safe response
1. Do not click the link or open unexpected attachments.
2. Check sender details carefully; display names can be faked.
3. Visit the official site using a known address or trusted bookmark.
4. Verify financial requests through a separate trusted channel.
5. Report suspicious messages to the organization's IT/security team.
6. If credentials were entered, change them through the official site and contact support/security promptly.

HTTPS encrypts a connection but does not prove a website is legitimate. The included URL checker is a simple heuristic only.
